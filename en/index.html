About

Neo LinearPad turns off trackpad acceleration on your MacBook. The pointer moves at a constant rate, at the sensitivity you choose.

Since macOS 15 Sequoia (September 2024), LinearMouse and similar apps can no longer turn off acceleration for some users.
Neo LinearPad works reliably on current macOS.

Get the straightforward, Windows-like feel, with more sensitivity range than you’ll ever need.
Works with Magic Trackpad too.

How to install (free trial)
  1. Click Download on this page, choose “30-day free trial”, and download the .dmg.
  2. Open the .dmg from your browser’s downloads.
  3. Drag Neo LinearPad into the Applications folder.
  4. Open Neo LinearPad. The first time, macOS asks you to confirm opening an app downloaded from the internet. Click Open.
  5. Click the icon at the top right of the screen, then turn on the switch.

Details

  • Scrolling, gestures and mouse settings are not affected at all.
  • Your sensitivity and other settings are kept when you quit the app.
  • No Accessibility or other permissions needed.
  • Turning the feature off with the switch in the app has the same effect as quitting, and the app stays in your login items.
  • Check for other apps that get in the way (easy)

    If another pointer app, such as LinearMouse, is running at the same time and interfering with Neo LinearPad, the app can tell you which one.

    1. Click the menu bar icon at the top right to open the app’s window.
    2. Click “Check for conflicting apps” at the bottom.
    3. While it’s checking, repeat whatever caused the problem (moving the pointer, switching windows, and so on).
    4. The interfering app appears below the button. (The check ends by itself after 10 minutes.)
    5. Quit that app, or otherwise keep it from running alongside Neo LinearPad.
    6. If it says “Couldn’t check”, you may be using a Mac account without administrator rights.
  • If it doesn’t start each time you open your Mac
    1. Click the Apple menu (the apple at the top left of the screen) and choose “System Settings…”.
    2. Click “General” in the list on the left.
    3. Click “Login Items & Extensions” (or “Login Items”).
    4. Look for Neo LinearPad in the “Open at Login” list at the top. If it’s there, turn on the switch to its right, and you’re done.
    5. If it isn’t, click the “+” button just below the list.
    6. In the window that opens, click “Applications” on the left, select Neo LinearPad, and click “Open” at the bottom right.
    7. Neo LinearPad is added to “Open at Login”. Make sure its switch is on. Neo LinearPad now starts each time you open your Mac.
  • If you have more than one Mac

    For personal use, one license key works on multiple Macs.

    1. Install Neo LinearPad (free trial) on the other Mac.
    2. On the Mac with the license, right-click the menu bar icon at the top right and choose “Use on Another Mac…”.
    3. Copy the license key shown.
    4. On the other Mac, right-click the menu bar icon and choose “Enter License Key…”.
    5. Paste the key and click “Activate”.

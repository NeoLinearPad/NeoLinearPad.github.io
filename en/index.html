About

Neo LinearPad turns off trackpad acceleration on your MacBook. The pointer moves in proportion to your finger movement, at the sensitivity you choose.

Since the release of macOS 15 Sequoia in September 2024, some users have been unable to disable trackpad acceleration with LinearMouse or similar apps.
Neo LinearPad works reliably on the latest macOS.

Enjoy the straightforward, Windows-like feel, with a wider sensitivity range than you’ll ever need.
Works with Magic Trackpad too.

Details

  • Scrolling, gestures and mouse settings are not affected at all.
  • Your sensitivity and other settings are kept even after you quit the app.
  • No Accessibility or other permissions needed.
  • Turning off the switch has the same effect as quitting the app, without removing it from your login items.
  • Easily check for conflicting apps

    If another pointer utility, such as LinearMouse, interferes with Neo LinearPad, you can use the built-in checker to identify it.

    1. Click the menu bar icon at the top right to open the app’s window.
    2. Click “Check for conflicting apps” at the bottom.
    3. While the check is running, repeat the action that caused the problem, such as moving the pointer or switching windows.
    4. The interfering app appears below the button. (The check ends by itself after 10 minutes.)
    5. Quit that app, or otherwise keep it from running alongside Neo LinearPad.
    6. If you see “Couldn’t check”, your Mac user account may not have administrator privileges.
  • If Neo LinearPad doesn’t open automatically when you start your Mac
    1. Click the Apple menu (the apple at the top left of the screen) and choose “System Settings…”.
    2. Click “General” in the list on the left.
    3. Click “Login Items & Extensions” (or “Login Items”).
    4. Look for Neo LinearPad in the “Open at Login” list at the top. If it’s there, turn on the switch to its right, and you’re done.
    5. If it isn’t, click the “+” button just below the list.
    6. In the window that opens, click “Applications” on the left, select Neo LinearPad, and click “Open” at the bottom right.
    7. Neo LinearPad is added to “Open at Login”. Make sure its switch is on. Neo LinearPad will now open automatically when you start your Mac.
  • If you have more than one Mac

    For personal use, one license key works on multiple Macs.

    1. Install the free trial of Neo LinearPad on your other Mac.
    2. On the Mac with the license, right-click the menu bar icon at the top right and choose “Use on Another Mac…”.
    3. Copy the license key shown.
    4. On the other Mac, right-click the menu bar icon and choose “Enter License Key…”.
    5. Paste the key and click “Activate”.

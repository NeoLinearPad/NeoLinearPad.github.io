Refund Policy

Within 14 days of purchase, you can get a refund for Neo LinearPad for any reason.

How to request a refund

To request a refund, email support@neolinearpad.com from the email address you used to make the purchase. If you’re writing from a different address, include the email address you used to make the purchase or your order number. If you have your order number, please include it.

After the refund

  • Polar, our reseller, issues the refund to your original payment method. The time it takes for the refund to appear depends on your card issuer or payment provider.
  • Your license key is disabled, and the app returns to its pre-purchase state.
  • Payments are processed in US dollars, so differences caused by exchange rates or card fees are not refunded.

After 14 days

We generally don’t issue refunds more than 14 days after purchase. If you’re having trouble with the app, please contact us.

Refund Policy

Within 14 days of purchase, you can get a refund for Neo LinearPad for any reason.

How to request a refund

Email support@neolinearpad.com from the address you used to buy, saying you’d like a refund. Include your order number if you have it.

After the refund

  • The money goes back to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy, our reseller. How long it takes to show up depends on your card issuer or payment provider.
  • The refunded license key is disabled, and the app goes back to how it was before you bought it.
  • Payments are processed in US dollars, so differences caused by exchange rates or card fees are not refunded.

After 14 days

As a rule, we don’t give refunds. If you’re having trouble, such as a bug, feel free to contact us.

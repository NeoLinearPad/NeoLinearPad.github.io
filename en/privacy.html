Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Neo LinearPad (“the app”) and this website handle information.

1. What the app doesn’t collect

The app doesn’t collect or send personal information such as keystrokes, screen contents, usage or location. Your settings (such as sensitivity) and the start date of your free trial are stored only on your Mac.

The conflicting-app check reads your Mac’s system log on that Mac only. Nothing it reads is sent anywhere.

2. What’s sent to check your license

When you activate a license key, and once a day after that, the app sends the following to Lemon Squeezy, our reseller, to confirm that the license is valid:

  • Your license key
  • An ID for each Mac (made by scrambling the Mac’s hardware ID one way, so the original can’t be worked out)

It doesn’t send your computer’s name, your name or similar information. When you’re offline, nothing is sent.

3. Purchase information

Lemon Squeezy handles purchases and payments. Your name, email address and payment details go to Lemon Squeezy and are handled under its privacy policy. The developer never sees your card number or other payment details.

4. Messages you send

Questions and feedback you send by email, and your email address, are used only to reply and to improve the app.

5. This website

This website doesn’t use cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub handles the technical records of each visit (such as IP addresses).

To see how many people visit, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It doesn’t use cookies, and counts things like pages viewed, referring sites and browser types without identifying individuals.

6. Changes

We may change this policy when needed. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
